# Project Name: High School Band Communications App

## Project Purpose
The high school band program stuggles with getting word out to parents about events, needs, and volunteer opportunites. The parents do not have a way to view any needs or opportunities to help. Easy, clear communication is essential for the band program to run.

## Description
 * Build an app that is a base for all communication between the band directors and parents of students
 * Use an app builder, for example [Glide](https://www.glideapps.com/) to facilitate
 * Create boards within the app that specify each section
   * Events
   * Volunteer needs/signup 
   * Chat
   * Calendar
   * Alerts
 * Roll out soft test to get feedback on functionality and user interface
   * Invite small group of parents to test

## Desired Results
 * Band directors can clearly and quickly express any needs relating to an event, needs, or volunteer help
 * Parents can view events, calendars, and sign up for volunteer opportunities
 * The app will be user friendly as established by soft test
 * The app will be the main source of communication between directors and parents
 * 100% useage from band directors, booster team, and parents of students in the band

 ## Exceptions
 Strictly for high school band, booster club, and parents of band members. App will exclude any color guard, dance, or cheer teams to keep calendar relevant to **band** only.
